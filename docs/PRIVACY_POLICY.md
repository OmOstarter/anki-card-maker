# Privacy Policy — 線上詞典助手 (Online Dictionary Helper)

_Last updated: 2026-07-08_

This extension does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the
developer or any third-party analytics service.

## What stays on your device
- **Settings** (enabled state, hotkey, selected deck/fields, tags, etc.) are
  stored locally using the browser's extension storage. They never leave your
  device except through the browser's own optional sync, if you enable it.
- **Dictionary lookups** are answered entirely from a dictionary bundled inside
  the extension. Lookups are performed offline and are **not** sent anywhere.

## Network connections (only when you act)
- **Add to Anki (optional):** if you choose to add a card, the note is sent to
  **your own local Anki** via AnkiConnect at `http://127.0.0.1:8765`
  (localhost). Nothing is sent to the developer.
- **Pronunciation audio (optional):** if you play audio, the word is sent to
  Youdao's public audio endpoint (`https://dict.youdao.com/dictvoice`) to
  retrieve the sound clip. No other information is included.

## What we do NOT do
- No analytics, telemetry, tracking, ads, or fingerprinting.
- No collection of browsing history or page content.
- No remote code execution.
